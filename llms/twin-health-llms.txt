# Twin Health

> Twin Health is a metabolic health company whose Whole Body Digital Twin uses AI and data from sensors and wearables to deliver personalized daily guidance and clinician-led care for type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, and obesity, sold through employers and health plans. It publishes no public developer API; members use the Twin Health mobile apps and the MyCare by Twin portal.
